Pipeline timeline

Simplified model: each stage takes one time step. With the pipeline, decoding starts once the queue holds the first two instructions (6 bytes); each decoded instruction leaves the queue exit and the following bytes move down (FIFO). In the real 8086 the BIU (fetch) and the EU (decode + execute) work in parallel, and the BIU refills the queue whenever at least 2 bytes are free.
